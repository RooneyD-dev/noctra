02 / TECHNOLOGY

A quieter kind of augmented.

N-01 is designed around restraint: the interface appears when it is useful and recedes when it is not. Choose a principle to read how it behaves.

See

Glanceable information stays in your natural field of view. Directions, timings and reference marks sit at the edge of attention — present when required, unnoticed when not.

PRACTICE 01 / GLANCE, DON’T STARE

Move

Controls are designed for movement and everyday use. A light tap, a turn of the head, a short voice cue — interactions that still hold together while you are walking, carrying or working.

PRACTICE 02 / BUILT FOR MOTION

Focus

The interface recedes when you do not need it. No persistent overlays, no feed, no badges — a quiet layer that steps back the moment your attention belongs somewhere else.

03 / FIELD NOTES

Built for the in-between.

Most of a day is spent moving between destinations and obligations. These are envisioned scenarios for how N-01 could sit inside them.

ENVISIONED USE CASES / CONCEPT SCENARIOS

On the move

Stay oriented, keep moving.

Directions and timely context while navigating a new city — the next turn, a platform change, a plan that just shifted — surfaced at the edge of view so your attention stays on the street.

In the work

Both hands, still informed.

Quiet prompts and shared visual references during a hands-on task — a spec, a checklist step, a marked-up image held beside the work instead of on a screen you have to put down.

After hours

Present, with room to drift.

Personal media and messages that stay discreet and optional — a short summary you can glance at, or leave entirely alone until morning.
